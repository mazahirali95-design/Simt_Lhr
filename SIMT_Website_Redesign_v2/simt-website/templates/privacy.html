This draft notice describes how SIMT handles information submitted through this website. It should be reviewed against your actual operating practices before public launch.

What we collect

When you contact SIMT, we collect information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, company, business stage, topic of interest and message. Consultation requests may include a preferred date.

Why we collect it

We use the information to receive and review your request, communicate with you and manage follow-up. We do not use submitted information as a public testimonial or client project without permission.

How it is stored

Contact records are stored in the website's database and, when configured, notifications may be sent through a third-party email provider. Authorized SIMT administrators can access submissions through a protected dashboard.

Retention and requests

SIMT should establish a retention period and an appropriate process for handling access or deletion requests before launch. Email {{ site_email }} with privacy questions.

SIMORA website assistant

SIMORA's initial guided assistant processes the text you enter to select a relevant response. This version does not save chat transcripts or ask for sensitive personal or financial information. Do not enter confidential business information into the chat.

WhatsApp and Calendly

Links to WhatsApp and Calendly open external services with their own privacy practices. If you continue through those services, their terms and privacy notices apply.

Third-party fonts

The website may request display fonts from Google Fonts. This can share connection information with the font provider. Review this choice before launch or replace the font references with suitable local/system fonts.

Cookies

The public website does not use advertising-tracking cookies. The protected administration area uses an essential authentication cookie when an administrator signs in.

Draft for review, not legal advice. Update this notice based on your actual hosting, email provider, data retention and local legal requirements before publishing.
